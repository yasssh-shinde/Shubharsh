## ✨ Features Included

1. **Royal 3D Gate & Wax Seal Reveal:**
   - Tap-to-open 3D royal doors with embossed golden wax monogram seal (`SH`).
   - Seamlessly triggers background music on first tap (satisfies mobile autoplay restrictions).

2. **Falling Floral Petals Canvas Animation:**
   - Soft rose and golden marigold petals drifting gently across the screen with natural wind physics.

3. **Background Music & Dual Audio Engine:**
   - Plays romantic instrumental wedding music.
   - Includes a built-in procedural Web Audio API Indian raga flute & tanpura harmonic synthesizer as a fallback, ensuring music plays even if offline or if external files are blocked.
   - Floating music pill with play/pause and animated equalizer visualizer bars.

4. **Auspicious Shloka & Invocation:**
   - `॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥` (With the divine blessings of Lord Ganesha).

5. **Interactive "Save The Date" Scratch Card:**
   - Realistic metallic gold foil scratch-off card that guests can scratch with their finger (touch) or mouse.
   - Instant "Tap here to reveal" button that confirms when the date is revealed.
   - Revealing the date unlocks the countdown and ceremony details.
   - Golden confetti celebration burst upon reveal.

6. **Live Countdown Timer:**
   - Real-time countdown tracking Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds until the wedding ceremony.

7. **Wedding Ceremony Details:**
   - A single event card for Saturday, 5 December 2026 at 12:13 PM.
   - Google Maps directions in the dedicated venue section.

8. **"Our Moments" Polaroid Gallery:**
   - Smooth horizontal swipe/scroll polaroid carousel with active dot indicators.
   - Fullscreen Lightbox modal on tap/click with zoom and captions.

## 🌐 How to Host for Free (To Share on WhatsApp)

### Option 1: Vercel (Fastest & 100% Free)
1. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and sign in.
2. Drag and drop the `Shubharsh` folder into Vercel.
3. You will instantly get a custom live link (e.g. `https://shubham-harshada-wedding.vercel.app`) to send to family and friends.

### Option 2: Netlify (Drag & Drop)
1. Go to [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
2. Drag and drop the `Shubharsh` folder.
3. Your wedding invitation website goes live instantly!

### Option 3: GitHub Pages
1. Push this folder to a GitHub repository.
2. Go to **Settings** → **Pages** → select `main` branch and `/root` folder → Save.
